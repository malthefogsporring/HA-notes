\section{Operads from monoidal categories}
\label{sec:envelope}
The purpose of this section is to summarize results of Haugseng-Kock which allow one to describe the operads (i.e. $\mathbb{F}$-operads) as certain symmetric monoidal categories equipped with a monoidal functor to $\mathbb{F}$. We will describe the theory for (symmetric) operads, i.e. $\mathbb{F}$-operads \cite{HaugsengKock}. The theory can also be extended to a large class of algebraic patterns\footnote{Specifically those that are \textit{sound} and \textit{extendable}} \cite{Barkan-Haugseng-Steinebrunner}.

The category of operads $Op$ is the subcategory of $Cat_{\infty/\mathbb{F}_*}$ spanned by operads and morphisms between them that preserve cocartesian lifts of inerts. Recall Lurie's definition of a symmetric monoidal category as a coCartesian fibration $p:C^{\tensor}\rightarrow \mathbb{F}_*$, and that a symmetric monoidal functor is a morphism in $Cat_{\infty,/\mathbb{F}_*}$ between symmetric monoidal categories that preserves all coCartesian edges. In this formulation, a symmetric monoidal category is in particular operad, and a monoidal functor is in particular an operad morphism, giving a faithful functor $U: SMonCat\xhookrightarrow{} Op$. This functor has a left adjoint, given by the \defn{symmetric monoidal envelope}. Informally, it takes an operad $\mathcal{O}^\tensor$ to a the category $\mathcal{O}^\tensor_{act}$ of active morphisms in $\mathcal{O}^\tensor$ with the symmetric monoidal product given by concatenation, which is sensible due to conditions 1 and 2 of \cref{def:operad}. Here, an active morphism in $O^\tensor$ means a morphism lying over an active morphism in $\mathbb{F}_*$.

To make the construction precise, define $Act(\mathbb{F}_*)$ as the full subcategory of $Ar(\mathbb{F}_*):=Fun(\Delta^1,\mathbb{F}_*)$ whose objects are active morphisms, and let \newline$s,t:Act(\mathbb{F}_*)\rightarrow \mathbb{F}_*$ denote the source and target morphisms, respectively. We note this construction gives a functor $Env:Op\rightarrow Cat_{\infty,/\mathbb{F}_*}$. By \cite{HA}[2.2.4] this factors through the subcategory $SMonCat$ and is a left adjoint to the forgetful functor $U:Op\rightarrow SMonCat$, which we record as the following theorem.

\begin{theorem}[\cite{HA} 2.2.4.4 and 2.2.4.9]\label{thm:env-functor}
The construction of the envelope gives a functor $Env:Op\rightarrow SMonCat$ left adjoint to $U:SMonCat\rightarrow Op$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{example}\label{ex:envelope-of-F}
For the terminal operad $id:\mathbb{F}_*\rightarrow \mathbb{F}_*$ we have \newline $Env(\mathbb{F}_*)\equiv Act(\mathbb{F}_*)$. We can identify its underlying category with $\mathbb{F}\simeq Act(\mathbb{F}_*)_{\langle 1 \rangle}$ and notice that the monoidal product is given by disjoint union of finite sets.
\end{example}

\cref{ex:envelope-of-F} demonstrates that $Env$ can be upgraded to a functor \newline$Env':Op\rightarrow SMonCat_{/{\mathbb{F}^{\sqcup}}}.$ On the other hand, given a monoidal functor $C^\tensor\rightarrow \mathbb{F}^{\sqcup}$, one can apply the forgetful functor to $Op$ then pull it back along the unique monoidal functor $\mathbb{F}_*\rightarrow \mathbb{F}^\sqcup$ in $Op$ to get an operad $U'(C^\tensor)\rightarrow \mathbb{F}_*$. This construction assembles to a functor $U':SMonCat_{/{\mathbb{F}^\sqcup}}\rightarrow Op$.

\begin{theorem}[\cite{HaugsengKock}]\label{thm:haugseng-kock}
The functor $Env'$ is left adjoint to $U'$. Furthermore, $Env'$ is fully faithful. 
\end{theorem}

Our hope is to use \cref{thm:haugseng-kock} as a definition for twofold operads, that is, to identify the comonad on the category of twofold symmetric monoidal categories corresponding to $Env\circ U$. Alternatively, Haugseng and Kock also give an explicit characterization of the subcategory of $SMonCat_{/{\mathbb{F}^\sqcup}}$ cut out by $Env'$ in \cite{HaugsengKock}[2.4.6] which we may attempt to replicate.
