\section{Next steps}\label{sec:whats-next}
This section will record questions and next steps for this project. Everything said here is speculative and unapologetically vague.

\begin{enumerate}[I.]
    \item A major goal is to compare the twofold monoidal categories of \cite{Bar24} with the duoidal categories of \cite{Torii24}. In particular, the category of twofold monoidal categories should be equivalent to the category of duoidal categories where the units are equivalent. This would addionally allow us to confirm the proposed universal properties of $\mathbb{D},\mathbb{E}$ and $\mathbb{H}$.
    \item A non-symmetric version of twofold monoidal categories exists, using certain directed graphs. We say a $[n,m]-$category is a twofold category that is an $E_n$-monoidal category with respect to the left tensor product and $E_m$-monoidal category with respect to the right tensor product. In upcoming work with Clark Barwick, we will show that the functor $SMonCat\rightarrow [\infty,n]-MonCat$ assigning to a symmetric monoidal category $C^\tensor$ the repeated $[\infty,n]-$monoidal structure $C^{\tensor,\tensor}$ has a left adjoint given by inverting the interchange map, and explore related results.
    \item As it stands, the description of $\mathbb{H}$ is somewhat esoteric. We would like to find a more natural description whose morphisms are easier to describe.
    \item We believe certain fibrations over $\mathbb{H}$ can give another description of twofold symmetric monoidal categories, and we would like to give this description. It would serve as an intermediate category between $\mathbb{F}^{op}\times \mathbb{F}$ and $\mathbb{E}$, and could be compared to duoidal categories and twofold monoidal categories in the sense of \cite{Bar24}. In particular, since $\mathbb{H}$ is inherently "twisted", the duoidal structure on a twofold monoidal category should be pulled back along natural map(s) $\mathbb{F}^{op}\times \mathbb{F}\rightarrow \mathbb{H}$. Similarly, it should be simple to identify a compound tensor category with its corresponding twofold symmetric monoidal category in this framework.
    \item As stated in \cref{sec:envelope}, we are interested in giving a definition of twofold operads. There are several potential approaches: as a fibration over $\mathbb{E}_*$ or $\mathbb{H}_*$, or as a subcategory of $2SMonCat_{/\mathbb{E}_*}$ or $2SMonCat_{/\mathbb{H}_*}$, suitably defined.
    \item We interested in twofold operads mainly because we believe the theory has interesting applications, which we would like to explore. For one, it will be possible to generalise the results in \cite{BeilinsonDrinfeld} to the $\infty-$categorical world. It is also our hope that the self-duality of twofold operads can give a natural description of Koszul duality. A major goal of the project going forward will be to understand Koszul duality for operads to understand if they can be described using twofold operads.
\end{enumerate}
