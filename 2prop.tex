\section{Twofold $\infty$-operads}
The goal of this section is to formulate a $2-$fold version of the equivalence of $Op_\infty$ with a full subcategory of the category $PROP_\infty$ of $\infty-$properads \cite{HaugsengKock}. We will start by giving an analogue of the notion of PROP.

Any symmetric monoidal category $(C,\tensor)$ can be canonically viewed as a twofold monoidal category where the two tensor products are the same. The following lemma makes this statement precise.

\begin{prop}
    Let $\pi:C^\tensor \rightarrow F_*$ be a symmetric monoidal category. Let $f:\mathbf{E_*}\rightarrow \mathbf{F_*}$ be the functor on points as in \cite{Bar18} Define $C^{\tensor,\tensor}$ as the pullback
\[
\begin{tikzcd}
{C^{\tensor,\tensor}} \pullback \arrow[d, "\pi'"'] \arrow[r, "f'"] & C^\tensor \arrow[d, "\pi"] \\
\mathbf{E_*} \arrow[r, "f"]                                      & \mathbf{F_*}                       
\end{tikzcd}\]
    Then $\pi'$ is a twofold monoidal category.
    \label{lem:twofold-from-onefold}
\end{prop}
\begin{proof}
    As coCartesian fibrations are preserved by pullbacks, $\pi'$ is a coCartesian fibration. It also satisfies the Segal condition as $\pi$ does.

    Now let $s,z:\mathbf{F_*}\rightarrow \mathbf{E_*}$ be the inclusions of discrete and completely disconnected graphs, and note they are sections of $f.$ This in turn induces functors $s',z':C^\tensor \rightarrow C^{\tensor,\tensor}$ which are sections of $f'$. It is then not hard to see $s',z'$ are pull-backs of $s,z$ along $\pi'$ respectively.
\end{proof}

Let $\pi:Cat_\infty^{\times,\times}\rightarrow E_*$ be the twofold monoidal category corresponding to $(Cat_\infty,\times,1)$ via the construction in \cref{lem:twofold-from-onefold}. Let us remark that a twofold monoidal category $C^{\underline{\oplus},\overline{\oplus}}$ is the same data as section of $\pi:Cat_\infty^{\times,\times}\rightarrow E_*$. We can therefore define $2CMon(Cat_{\infty})$ as the full subcategory of the category of sections of $\pi:Cat_\infty^{\times,\times}\rightarrow E_*$ spanned by twofold functors between twofold monoidal categories.
