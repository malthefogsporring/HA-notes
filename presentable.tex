\section{How the hell do you prove that the category of commutative monoids in a symmetric monoidal category is presentable (but for real tho how do you do it)??}
This section is devoted to answering the question in the section title. The purpose is to figure out how the hell to generalize this to twofold monoidal categories.

Throughout, let $p:C^\tensor\rightarrow F_*$ be a symmetric monoidal category. Remark that a section $s:F_*\rightarrow C^\tensor$ of $p$ is automatically an operad map (as inerts of $C^\tensor$ are just maps lying over inerts).
